Pathweft 1.1.92 puts other programs' Send with entries in the Transfer group of the right-click menu (MENU-11).

- Right-click a file → Transfer now holds Send with Tailscale… (and any other program's Send with entry) beside Copy to and Move to. Before, it was at the bottom of More actions.
- Help (F1) lists it.

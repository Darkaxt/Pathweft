Pathweft 1.1.95 keeps the index working in administrator view (index service, IDX-03 and IDX-08).

- Switching to administrator view no longer breaks indexing of other drives and network shares (T:, H:, mapped shares). The index service scanned those folder roots with the most recent token of their owner, which after the switch was the administrator view's elevated token. That token belongs to another Windows logon session without your drive mappings, so rescans failed with "path not found". Folder roots are now always scanned with your standard token.
- Which folders each view may open is now remembered separately for the normal and the administrator view, so one view's answers are never used for the other.
- Help (F1) and What's new describe it.

Pathweft 1.1.108 moves the preview inside the pane (PREVIEW-41, CORE-36).

- With one pane, the preview opens inside the pane, under its tabs. The tabs keep their place and no longer wrap into another row when the preview opens, so the list doesn't move under the pointer; the path bar and the files move over to make room. The preview is a card with rounded corners; drag its left edge to size it.
- With two panes, each pane shows a picture-in-picture preview of its own selection, both at the same time, each with its own pin, floating window and close. Before, only the focused pane's selection was shown.
- A double-click opens the file its first click was on, even if the files moved under the pointer between the two clicks.
- The preview's top no longer moves the window (it is no longer at the window's top); the empty part of the tab row does, as before.
- Help and What's new describe it.

Pathweft 1.1.112 puts the new-tab button after the last tab (TABS-09).

- The + that opens a new tab sits right after the last tab, on the last tab's row, with one row of tabs or several. Before, wrapped tabs left it at the top right of the header.
- Help and What's new describe it.

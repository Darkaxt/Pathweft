Pathweft 1.1.107 adds a Loop button to the media preview (PREVIEW-40).

- Videos and songs in the preview pane, Space preview and the floating preview have a Loop button beside Mute. On, playback starts over at the end without pausing; off, it stops at the end as before. The choice stays for the next previews until you turn it off.
- Help and What's new describe it.

Pathweft 1.1.70 fixes a crash in apps that open folders through Windows, and works better with Tresorit Drive.

- Fixed: opening a folder from another app (for example JDownloader) while Pathweft was updating, or opening This PC from an app, could crash that app. Pathweft's folder handler now passes these requests to Windows correctly (SHELL-05).
- Tresorit Drive (issue #8):
  - The item menu shows Tresorit's own commands, Sharing and members and Copy Tresorit Path, as Explorer does.
  - Deleting a tresor says what Tresorit does: only its Owner can delete it, and it is then deleted for every member and can't be restored. Pathweft deletes the tresor as a whole or not at all, and never deletes its contents first. A refusal says that only the Owner can delete or rename it.
  - Deleting files and folders inside a tresor says they go to Tresorit's deleted files and can be restored in the Tresorit app, instead of Windows' "permanently delete".
  - The drive's space is labelled as your Tresorit account's storage, not a disk.
- Specification 1.28 records this.

Update from Pathweft's own updater or run the installer, which requests Windows administrator consent. Pathweft binaries and installer are unsigned; verify the published files with SHA256SUMS.txt and release-manifest.json.

Source: `ee0d9a1` (tag `v1.1.94`), product source `1d7e814`. Spec 1.57, plan S65.

## Scope

- CORE-30 (owner, after the 1.1.93 report: "who cares about the disk analysis be desynced by a couple of minutes at worst? ... If I want a refresh, allow me to press F5"): Analyze space is a snapshot. A change on disk no longer re-reads or re-measures anything; it only marks the analysis out of date, and the footer says so and points at F5. F5 refreshes the whole analysis from the index service, measuring on disk only folders the index does not answer, and keeps the rows and selection in place while it does (no Pending or Measuring flashes). Ctrl+R and Try again still measure everything on disk. This supersedes CORE-29's watcher-driven refresh. Help and What's new describe it.

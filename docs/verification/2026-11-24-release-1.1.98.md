# Release 1.1.98 verification (S69, IDX-20, IDX-21)

Source: `aa16b6b` (tag `v1.1.98`). Plan S69, spec 1.59, index-service SPEC IDX-20 and IDX-21.

## Scope

The owner: "Why is browsing M: so slow and lagging? Isn't everything indexed?"

Diagnosis (installed 1.1.97, read-only):
- `pwindex sizes 'M:\Books'` answered "(not indexed)". The service runs as SYSTEM and knows the share only by its UNC path, and Pathweft sent the drive-letter path, so every pane size, search and analysis on M: walked the network.
- The service log showed every folder store rebuilt from scratch at each service start. The share took 1,796,829 ms (34,698 entries).

Changes:
- IDX-21. A mapped drive letter is sent as its share's UNC path for folder sizes, queries and Analyze space coverage, and results come back under the letter.
  - This applies only when the service has a store for the share and none for the letter. T:, a Tresorit drive indexed as `T:`, is unchanged.
  - A store being rescanned counts as indexed for Analyze space.
- IDX-20. The index service saves each folder root's index (SYSTEM and administrators only):
  - after a full scan, at most every 10 minutes while it changes, and at shutdown.
  - At start the saved index answers at once, and the root is rescanned in the background.
  - A damaged image is discarded and the root is scanned. Removing the root deletes its saved index.
  - UNC roots are listed with 8 threads.

## Limits recorded

- **Rename:** PowerRename's EXIF/XMP tokens are not ported.
- **Checksum:** NanaZip's rarer hashes (GOST, Whirlpool, EDON-R and others) are not included.
- **OCR:** language availability depends on the Windows OCR languages installed on the PC. Recognition runs on the preview bitmap, at most 2000 px on its long side.
- **File Locksmith:**
  - Without administrator rights, other users' and elevated processes may be hidden.
  - It never ends processes or closes handles, by specification.
  - In-use detection after a failed transfer reads the system error text.
- **Compress and email:** verified up to the MAPI call; no message was sent.
- **Private desktops:** the private window station was denied on this host (ACCESS_DENIED even from an unsandboxed Medium shell), so the tools scenario ran on a private desktop of WinSta0, as space-ui did in 1.1.75.

Pathweft index service: licences of the Rust crates linked into pathweft-indexd.exe and pwindex.exe
(versions from Cargo.lock; each crate is used under its MIT licence option). rusqlite, libsqlite3-sys
and their dependencies bitflags, fallible-iterator, fallible-streaming-iterator and smallvec are linked into
pathweft-indexd.exe only, with SQLite 3.54.1 compiled in from libsqlite3-sys (public domain, notice at the end).
